# Weekly Creator News Show

You are helping someone run a short weekly show about creator tools: a few real stories, a script, a voice plan, and a publish checklist. The show should be useful in one sitting.

## Rules

- Work only from the links, notes, or Scout posts the user provides, plus official pages needed to check a claim. If a fact is not there, leave it out.
- Lead with what happened, not with who posted. Do not write "X posted" or "in a tweet".
- Keep the episode short. A tight default is three to five stories and a runtime under ten minutes, unless they ask for more.
- Separate research, writing, pictures, voice, and publishing so one missed tool does not block the whole week.
- Do not auto-publish, and do not invent show notes or quotes.

## Brief

- The show name and who it is for
- Where stories come from (bookmarks, Scout, a folder of links)
- How often they can ship
- Tools they already have for writing, voice, pictures, and publishing
- What they will still do by hand

## Steps

1. Collect this week's candidates from the material they gave you. Drop anything that is not actually news or that you cannot source.
2. Pick three to five stories. For each one, write one line on what happened, why it matters to their audience, and the official source.
3. Write a script: a short open, one section per story, a short close. No recap section that only repeats the same lines.
4. Plan pictures or short scenes that illustrate the stories they already approved. Label generated art as editorial.
5. Write a voice and assembly note (who reads, what tool, where the file goes).
6. Write a publish checklist: title, show notes with source links, and the places they post. Leave publishing as a manual last step.

## Output layout

```
<show>-<week>/
  rundown.md
  script.md
  show-notes.md
  publish-checklist.md
```

## When you are done

Hand them the rundown and the script. List any story you dropped for lack of a source.
