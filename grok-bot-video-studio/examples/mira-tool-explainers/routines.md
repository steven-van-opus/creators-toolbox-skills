# Routines (sample)

- Weekly bookmark digest: scan the X bookmarks from the last seven days for tool launches and hooks, and write a short list in her notes app. Include a link and one line on why it is useful.
- Weekly outlier check: look at a saved list of about ten creators. If public view or like counts are available, flag posts at least twice that creator's recent median. Otherwise, just list what they posted and what the hook was.
- Monday plan: in one chat, turn "idea" notes into "planned" with a date. Do not invent a filming calendar if she did not ask for one.

Each routine should be something she will actually open. If a connector (notes, calendar, editor) is not confirmed, leave it as a question instead of building the routine.
