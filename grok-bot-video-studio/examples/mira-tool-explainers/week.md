# Mira's week (sample)

Mira is a fictional creator who posts one or two short tool explainers a week, has a full-time job, bookmarks posts on X, and edits in a browser-based editor. This is a sample plan, not a live account.

- Monday: planning session. Review last week, pick one or two ideas, put a date on them.
- Midweek: one research digest from her X bookmarks and a short list of creators she already follows. Save useful hooks as notes, not as scripts to copy.
- After a recording: ask the Bot to create the editor project, cut silences, and match the style of her last explainer.
- Publish day: she writes the title, then uses a checklist for the description and the post.

She writes scripts by hand. The Bot only advises on structure when she asks.
