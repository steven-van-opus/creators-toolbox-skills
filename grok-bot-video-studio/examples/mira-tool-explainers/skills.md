# Skills (sample)

- /save-idea: save a link, a hook, or a screenshot note into her ideas list, with the source URL.
- /hook-advisor: suggest two or three frames for a video, using her own past scripts first and her hook notes second. Never paste another creator's caption.
- /edit-handoff: start a project in her editor, attach the reference (one of her own videos), and write cut-silence plus style-match notes.
- /publish-checklist: title ideas, a description outline, and the places she actually posts. Do not auto-publish in this sample.

Confirm each editor and notes connector before treating these as live.
