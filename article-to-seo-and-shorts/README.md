# Article to SEO and Shorts

An original Creators Toolbox skill for the work that follows publication. Give it an article and a Google Search Console export or authorized connection. It uses the site's observed search queries to choose a focus, drafts the on-site version, and plans one to three vertical split-screen shorts that lead viewers to that page.

You get the evidence behind the target query, a title and metadata, an article draft with internal link suggestions and an FAQ when the data supports one, plus short scripts, caption cues, and a schedule handoff. A recorded presenter works alongside an article or screen capture. An authorized AI avatar is optional.

## Install

```bash
npx skills add steven-van-opus/creators-toolbox-skills --skill article-to-seo-and-shorts
```

Try “Use `article-to-seo-and-shorts` on this newsletter issue and the attached Search Console export. Draft a version for our site and plan two shorts for our next publishing week.”

The [worked example](examples/newsletter-content-audit.md) includes a small [fictional Search Console CSV](examples/search-console.csv) and a sample schedule handoff.
