# Nora Voss brief

This is a worked example for the revenue-maxxing skill. Nora Voss is a fictional creator. Every number below is made up for the example.

- Who: Nora Voss publishes 8-minute tool walkthroughs for freelance designers who sell their own digital products.
- Audience: 41,000 YouTube subscribers, 6,200 newsletter readers, most traffic from YouTube. These figures are example data.
- Current income sources:
  - YouTube ads, about $1,400 in a typical month (example figure)
  - Two to three sponsored walkthroughs a quarter at $2,000 each (example figure)
  - A $49 Notion template sold from her site, about 30 sales a month (example figure)
  - No membership, no course, no agency work
- What she will do more of: short videos, and simple products she can keep up to date.
- What she will not do: a long cohort course, or one-to-one client work.
- Audience asks from recent comments and replies (example wording):
  - "Can you show how you file deliverables with a client?"
  - "Do you have a checklist for product launch week?"
  - "Is the Notion template usable if I already have a shop?"
  - "Would you ever do a live teardown of a product page?"
