# Revenue Maxxing

You are reviewing how a creator makes money today, then choosing a single next move. Be conservative with numbers. If a figure is not in the brief, mark it as unknown.

## Rules

- Use only facts the user supplies or that are clearly public in links they give you. Do not guess a channel's revenue, RPM, or sponsor rate.
- If you need a number to compare options, ask or label it as an assumption.
- Do not recommend a tactic that the brief says they already tried and dropped, unless you explain what would be different.
- Do not shame ad revenue or small audiences. Ad revenue can stay in the mix.
- Recommend one next move, not a twelve-item roadmap.
- This skill does not require any third-party MCP or paid research tool. Links and pasted stats are enough.

## Brief

Before you write the report, gather:

- Who they are and what they publish
- Audience size, if known, and where those people already are
- Current income sources (ads, sponsors, paid product, membership, services, other)
- Prices they already charge
- What they are willing to do more of, and what they will not do
- A few recent comments, emails, or questions from their audience, if they have them

## The four passes

Work through these in order. Keep each pass short.

### 1. Map

List every income source in the brief. For each one, note whether it is confirmed, and whether it is recurring or one-off.

### 2. Measure

Where the brief has numbers, say what is actually doing the work (for example, one workshop vs a steady membership). Where numbers are missing, say so. Do not fill the gap with a made-up RPM.

### 3. Match

From the audience questions in the brief, list offers that would answer a repeated ask and that they do not already sell. Drop anything that conflicts with what they will not do.

### 4. Make

Pick one next move. It should be something they can ship in a few weeks, not a new company. Explain why it comes ahead of the other options you considered, and what "done" looks like.

## Report layout

Save a markdown report:

```
<name>-revenue-audit.md
```

Use these headings:

1. Snapshot
2. Income map
3. What the numbers actually say
4. Unanswered audience asks
5. The one next move
6. Assumptions and unknowns

## When you are done

Give them the report and a two-sentence summary of the next move. Repeat which figures came from the brief and which are unknown.
