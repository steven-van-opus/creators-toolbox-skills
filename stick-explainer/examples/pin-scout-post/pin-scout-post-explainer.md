# Pin a Scout post

A sample stick-explainer for Creators Toolbox. A figure pins a short news card onto a feed board, then steps back as the card stays in place.

## Product and action

- Product: Scout publishes short news posts about creator tools.
- Action: a person pins a new post onto their feed.

## Metaphor (chosen)

A cork board and a single pin. The figure holds a small card, presses a pin through the corner, and the card hangs straight. It is physical, and the result is visible without a caption.

Other pitches that were not chosen: a tray of cards sliding into a mail slot; a figure stamping "posted" on a card.

## Storyboard

1. Idle: the figure stands beside an empty board, card in one hand, pin in the other, shifting weight.
2. Start: they lift the card to the upper left of the board.
3. Mid: the pin lines up with the corner of the card.
4. Mid: they press. The card flattens against the board.
5. Result: the card hangs. A small title bar is readable. The figure's hands come away.
6. Return: they step back to the idle stance. The card stays put. No pop.

## Label

"A person pins a Scout news card onto a feed board, then steps back."

## Implementer notes

- Size: 400 by 300, with padding so the raised card is not cropped.
- Trigger: play once on click or when the scene scrolls into view.
- Loop: only the idle breath loops. The pin action plays once per trigger.
- Do not cover the figure's face with the card. Keep the title bar inside the card.
