# Ideas

Audience: people who make short videos and are choosing an editor. Question: what do public starting prices actually look like across well-known AI video editors listed on Creators Toolbox?

1. Starting published price for five named editors, taken from each product's public pricing page, as a simple bar chart. Data need: the current starting plan name and price, plus the date checked. Easy to source.
2. How those same pages describe their free tier (none, trial, or a lasting free plan). A compact table graphic. Also easy to source.
3. A timeline of when each editor publicly listed a new plan this year. Only use this if the public pages actually show dated plan changes. Otherwise drop it.

Chosen: idea 1, with idea 2 as a small footnote under the bars if there is room.
