# Harborline directions

Three original directions written for the example brief. The chosen direction for the sample files is Direction 3.

## Direction 1, wordmark

Draw "Harborline" in a medium-contrast sans with slightly open counters, and cut a shallow notch in the stem of the H so it reads as a horizon line. Palette: ink `#1B2430` on cream `#F4EFE6`. Risk: the notch disappears below 24px, so a favicon would need a heavier H.

## Direction 2, monogram

A two-letter HL monogram inside a rounded rectangle, with the crossbar of the H continuing into the L as one line. Palette: deep teal `#0F4C4C` and cream `#F4EFE6`. Risk: it can look like a hotel badge if the rectangle is too tall.

## Direction 3, symbol plus wordmark (chosen)

A small circular mark made from two stacked arcs and a short vertical, like a pier meeting a quiet waterline, sitting to the left of a simple "Harborline" wordmark. Palette: tide `#0F4C4C` and sand `#F4EFE6`, with the wordmark in ink `#1B2430` on light backgrounds. Risk: if the arcs get decorative, it starts to look like a sunset logo. Keep them as two strokes of equal weight.
