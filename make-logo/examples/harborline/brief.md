# Harborline brief

This is a worked example for the make-logo skill. Harborline is a fictional newsletter. Do not treat it as a real company.

- Name: Harborline
- What it is: A weekly newsletter that tracks what independent coffee roasters are changing in their shops and on their menus.
- Who it is for: Roaster owners and managers who already have a shop, not first-time cafe openers.
- Where the mark will live first: A web header and a square avatar for the newsletter archive.
- Color or typography limits: No bright orange, no script lettering, no coffee-cup clip art.
- What it must not look like: A shipping company, a maritime insurance firm, or a generic steaming mug.
