# Match

A sample for the journalist-pitch skill. The article titles below are illustrative. Before a real pitch, replace them with live public pieces and live counts from the catalog.

## Beat

New AI video tools and how quickly they are showing up for creators.

## Data we can offer

A public count of tools tagged for AI video on Creators Toolbox, with the month they were listed, pulled from public product pages. The chart only uses listed-at dates and public pricing labels (Free, Free plan, Paid). It does not use private traffic or revenue.

## Illustrative matches (replace before sending)

1. A week-in-review about new video models. Gap: how many finished products, not models, actually listed for creators that month.
2. A funding story about one editor. Gap: how crowded the public directory already is around that category.

## First pitch

Start with the week-in-review, if that piece is real and recent. The offer is a simple bar chart of new AI video listings by month, plus the public table behind it.

## Unknowns

- The live count and date range have to be pulled at send time. Do not invent a number here.
- Confirm the reporter's current beat and email from a public page.
