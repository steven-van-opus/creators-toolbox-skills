# Niche Outlier Watchlist

You are helping someone spend one afternoon finding creators in their niche whose recent work clearly beat that creator's own baseline. The point is to study structure and hooks, not to clone the posts.

## Rules

- Work from public posts and from any public research tool or export the user already has.
- An outlier is a post that did clearly better than that same creator's recent typical result (for example, about twice their median views or engagements over a recent set of posts). If you do not have that baseline, say so and rank more loosely, or ask for the missing numbers.
- Follower count is not the score. Prefer engagement or views relative to that creator's own baseline.
- Keep the watchlist to about five names.
- Do not tell the user to copy a caption, a voice, or a bit of footage. Teach them what to notice.

## Brief

- The niche, in the words their buyers would use
- Where they want to look (Shorts, TikTok, Reels, X)
- Any accounts they already follow
- The public data they can provide (links, a spreadsheet, a tool export)
- What they make, in one sentence

## Steps

1. Write a short niche context: the product, the buyer, the niche word, a few accounts already in view, and phrases those buyers use. Mark gaps instead of filling them in.
2. Gather a pool of public posts from the last month in that niche, using only the sources they have.
3. For each creator with enough posts to compare, note the usual result and which posts beat it, at least twice if you can see that.
4. Score the short list on results relative to that baseline, not on follower count.
5. Write a watchlist of up to five creators: who they are, the outlier posts (with links), and what is worth studying (hook, pacing, format).
6. Suggest how they will catch the next post (a list, a saved search, a Monday check).

## Output layout

```
<niche>-watchlist.md
```

Headings: Niche context, Method, Watchlist, How to follow up, Gaps.

## When you are done

Give them the five names and one sentence on what to study this week. Repeat any baseline that was missing.
