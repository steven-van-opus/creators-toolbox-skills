# AI video editing: watchlist template

A sample for the niche-outlier-watchlist skill. Replace every account and number with a live public check.

## Niche context

- Product: a weekly newsletter about creator tools, including AI video editors
- Buyer: people who already film talking-head or screen videos and want a faster edit
- Niche word: "AI video editing"
- Already in view: not specified in this sample
- Buyer phrases: "edit while I sleep", "reference a viral video", "cut silences"

## Method

Look at public posts from the last 30 days in that niche. For any creator with at least eight public posts in that window, treat an outlier as a post with at least twice that creator's median public views (or likes, if views are hidden). If the baseline cannot be counted, say so and do not force the 2x rule.
